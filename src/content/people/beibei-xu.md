bio_en: Associate Professor Beibei Xu conducts interdisciplinary research in agricultural remote sensing, computer vision, and smart agriculture. Her work addresses complex perception, intelligent modeling, and precision management in agricultural production, with a focus on multi-source agricultural data fusion, crop growth status simulation, nutrient diagnosis, and intelligent decision-making methods. In recent years, she has led and participated in multiple national and international research projects, including a sub-task of a pilot project supported by the Ministry of Education of China, projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, innovation engineering projects of the Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences, and research projects supported by the United States Department of Agriculture (USDA). She has published 17 SCI/EI-indexed papers in international journals such as Computers and Electronics in Agriculture, which have received more than 1,100 citations. She holds three authorized patents and two software copyrights. She currently serves as a Guest Editor for the international SCI journals Frontiers in Plant Science, Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence, Agronomy, and Agriculture.
